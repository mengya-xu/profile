Research Profile

Health geographer and spatial epidemiologist whose research integrates GIScience, spatial data science, mobility analytics, and GeoAI to examine how built, social, and environmental conditions shape population health, aging, and health disparities. Current work focuses on environmental exposures, dementia, and cardiometabolic health using large-scale cohorts, Medicare claims, mobility, and geospatial data.
